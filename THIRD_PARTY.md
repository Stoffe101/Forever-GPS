# Third-party code and data

Forever-GPS reuses parts of four MIT-licensed WoW Forever addons. Why these parts were chosen over the alternatives is in [docs/REUSE.md](docs/REUSE.md).

- **Pinned copies:** the exact upstream files are kept in `third_party/`, with their hashes in `third_party/THIRD_PARTY.lock.json`.
- **Check:** `python -I tools/third_party.py verify` checks them, and CI runs it.
- **Licenses in the addon:** every upstream license ships in `Interface/AddOns/ForeverGPS/Licenses/`.

None of these addons is needed, loaded or detected. Nothing of theirs is registered: no minimap button, slash command, global or saved variable.

## CraftProfit

- **Repository:** https://github.com/Renaud-Sto/CraftProfit
- **Commit:** `79e8eda548dd49b4fdbd530d914419076c30b2ab`
- **License:** MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Sto. Shipped as `Licenses/CraftProfit-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Files used:**
  - `CraftProfit/AHAdapter.lua`
  - `docs/probe-findings.md` (its in-game measurements F1, F2, F5 and F13)
- **Used in:**
  - `Core/Market.lua`: the full scan.
  - `Core/MarketSearch.lua`: targeted searches.

| From CraftProfit | Changes in Forever-GPS |
| --- | --- |
| Reading the full scan only after the client's event stops arriving, ignoring the events after a read, and ending the scan when the window closes, by a token | Rewritten in Forever-GPS's style. The scan waits until the row count is also stable, ends with a reason after 30 seconds with no answer or 60 seconds of events, and rereads once if rows arrived during the read |
| A stack's buyout divided by its count | Kept |
| `pcall` around each read, so a failing read can never leave a scan stuck | Kept |
| The secret-value checks | Kept |
| Price-sorted targeted searches through `SendSearchQuery`, waiting for `IsThrottledMessageSystemReady` | Kept |
| Reading commodity and item results, and whether a result list was complete | Kept |
| Ignoring duplicate result events | Kept |
| | Searches run as a queue, with a 6-second limit per item |
| | Results are stored as price ladders in Forever-GPS's own market store |

Not used: the buy-quantity preset, the chart and history views, the price store, the UI, the slash commands and the minimap button.

## ForeverNet

- **Repository:** https://github.com/thewoolfi/ForeverNet
- **Commit:** `10a81d9d0c01a869a1789b1a18100ee08a4dda01`
- **License:** MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Andrew Woolfi. Shipped as `Licenses/ForeverNet-LICENSE.txt`.
- **File used:** `Market.lua` (`M.Quote`).
- **Used in:** `Core/Quote.lua`.
- **Taken:** the price of buying a quantity:
  - Cheapest first along the price tiers for commodities.
  - A bounded 0/1 covering knapsack for whole auctions, with an approximate fallback past the bound.
  - The result fields: covered, remaining, surplus, partial and approximate.
- **Changes:**
  - Works on Forever-GPS's compact price ladders.
  - A merchant's unlimited stock is added as a tier.
  - A missing price is "unknown", never zero.
- **Not used:** the networking, requests, queue board, themes, snapshot store, UI and everything else.

## profit

- **Repository:** https://github.com/ExcEssIv3/profit
- **Commit:** `823413b68211d37321501d4fc556a89e1b5ed426`
- **License:** MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 seth. Shipped as `Licenses/profit-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Files used:**
  - `Data/Recipes.lua`
  - `Data/Items.lua` (merchant-sold reagents only)
  - `tools/build_recipes.py`, kept for reference to show how the data was made
  - `tools/vendor_items.txt`, kept for reference
- **Used in:** `Data/Recipes.lua`, generated by `tools/recipes_import.py`.
- **Taken:** for 2,486 recipes, what each makes, its reagents, the skill it can be learned at, the skill where it turns yellow and grey, and how it is learned (trainer, recipe item, or neither known). Also which reagents merchants sell without limit, with the client's listed price.
- **Changes:**
  - Each recipe is packed into one string.
  - Recipes are keyed by the client's skill line ID.
  - Names are dropped.
  - Merchant prices are marked as listed until a merchant is seen.
- **Data license:** profit generated this data from the WoW Forever client's own tables, build 1.60.1.70790. The MIT license covers seth's generator, the layout and his hand-made lists. The facts belong to the game. They are used as facts the client already holds and are not claimed to be MIT or Forever-GPS's. See [docs/REUSE.md](docs/REUSE.md).
- **Not used:** disenchanting data, item names, the UI, the tooltips and the recording code.

## Easy Controller - Forever

- **Repository:** https://github.com/moust4ki/EasyControllerWowForever
- **Commit:** `cf2ad94a33efd50bbd9b36055b6d8f111ac33233` (version 1.20.1)
- **License:** MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 moust4ki. Shipped as `Licenses/EasyController-LICENSE.txt`. Its `ConsolePort.lua` and `textures/consoleport` (Artistic License 2.0, after ConsolePort) and its `Dict_*.lua` (CC-BY-SA-4.0) are under other licenses: nothing was taken from them.
- **Files used:** `ConfigWindow.lua`, read for how it drives its own window with a controller on Forever.
- **Used in:** `Core/Pad.lua`. No code was copied; the approach was followed and written anew.

| From Easy Controller | Changes in Forever-GPS |
| --- | --- |
| While its window is open, the window's buttons are priority override bindings (`SetOverrideBindingClick`) to hidden buttons, cleared when it closes | Kept. Bindings come from the player's profile, with modifier chords, and every action is a click on a hidden `ForeverGPSPad_<action>` button that hears down and up |
| A button already held when the window opens is bound only once released (`IsKeyDown`), so its release stays the game's | Kept |
| Learning a binding: a frame with `EnableKeyboard`, `SetPropagateKeyboardInput(false)` and `EnableGamePadButton`, a 10-second limit, the back button alone cancels | Kept, with Escape also cancelling and a held modifier making a chord |
| Everything that binds checked against `InCombatLockdown` | Kept, and added: a secure state handler (`SecureHandlerStateTemplate`, `RegisterStateDriver` on `[combat]`) drops the window's bindings the moment combat starts, and `PLAYER_REGEN_DISABLED` drops them just before. They come back after combat if the window is still open |

Not used: its input methods, wheels, reticle, dictionaries, keyboards, chat, glyph textures, profiles and everything outside the config window.

Also read, and not used: **ConsolePort** (https://github.com/seblindfors/ConsolePort, Artistic License 2.0). Its keyboard would have fitted, but its license asks more of a Modified Version than a small built-in keyboard warrants. Forever-GPS's keyboard (`UI/PadKeyboard.lua`) is its own.
